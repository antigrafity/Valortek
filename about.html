About Valortek

Business Technology, From One Supplier

PT. Valortek Ratanika Utama is a Jakarta-based technology company. We supply devices and software, build custom systems, and provide the services that keep them running, for businesses across Indonesia.

Our Story

Why Valortek Exists

Buying technology is harder than it should be. One vendor sells the devices, another builds the software, a third installs the network, and nobody owns the result when something doesn't work.

We bring those pieces together. We sell the hardware and software, build what isn't available off the shelf, and connect it all, so you deal with one company from quote to support.

What makes us different: we are not tied to one brand or one product line. When a new kind of technology becomes useful to our clients, it joins our catalog.
