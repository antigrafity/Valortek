Introduction

Adapted to the realities on the ground in Indonesia

Valortek Ratanika Utama is a company focused on the development and implementation of Information Technology, specifically in the field of Intelligence Information Technology and Intelligence Equipment (Matsusintel).

We are keenly aware that Indonesia possesses a rich diversity — social, political, and cultural — across all dimensions of national life. Based on our experience supporting various intelligence operations, we have concluded that intelligence technology imported from abroad often cannot be used optimally without significant adaptation to real conditions on the ground in Indonesia.

From this foundation, we have conducted extensive Research and Development in collaboration with various parties since 1995, giving rise to a range of Intelligence Information Technology Systems and Equipment that are suited to Indonesian conditions and capable of providing genuine solutions in support of intelligence operations.
