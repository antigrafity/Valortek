About Valortek

We Build Technology Around Real Problems

PT. Valortek Ratanika Utama is a technology company based in Jakarta. We design and build applications, data platforms, monitoring systems, and automation for businesses across Indonesia.

Our Story

Why We Work the Way We Do

Many technology projects fail for a simple reason: they start with a product and then look for a problem to solve. The result is software that people avoid and reports nobody reads.

We do it the other way around. We spend time understanding how the work is actually done, then choose the technology, whether that is a new application, a mobile app, sensors, a dashboard, or simply connecting the systems you already have.

What makes us different: we don't resell one software package or push one vendor. We build what fits, hand over everything we make, and stay around to improve it for as long as you need us.
