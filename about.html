About Valortek

We Help Organizations Understand Their Own Data

PT. Valortek Ratanika Utama is a Jakarta-based company specializing in data platforms, geospatial solutions, analytics, and visualization.

Our Story

Built Around One Question: "What Is Really Happening?"

Every organization we meet has the same frustration. The information exists somewhere, in an application, a spreadsheet, a field report, or a map, but nobody can see it together. Valortek was founded to solve that problem.

We specialize in the part of technology that turns raw information into understanding: collecting and cleaning data, adding location context, analyzing it, and presenting it through dashboards, maps, and 3D views that people actually use.

What makes us different. We don't sell a single software package. We look at the data you already have, choose the tools that fit it, and build a solution your team can run and extend after we hand it over.
