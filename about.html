Who We Are

A Solutions & Services Provider Focused On Cybersecurity

Valortek is a technology solution provider focused on cybersecurity and IT security services. Our approach centers on end-to-end solutions and services: assessment, consulting, solution design, implementation, system integration, security operations, managed services, maintenance, and continuous improvement.

Products and technology from our principals are positioned as technology components and enablers for building customer solutions — not as the primary focus of what we offer. This lets us build value through professional services, system integration, managed services, support, and lifecycle services.

We help organizations build, secure, monitor, operate, and improve the security and resilience of their digital infrastructure, adapting to the demands of an evolving threat landscape.

Our focus is not on selling products individually, but on understanding customer needs, designing solutions, integrating technology, operating systems, and providing support and continuous improvement.
