About Valortek

We Help Organizations Understand Their Own Data

PT. Valortek Ratanika Utama is a Jakarta-based company specializing in data platforms, geospatial solutions, analytics, and visualization.

Our Story

It Started With One Question: "What Is Actually Going On?"

We kept hearing the same complaint from clients. The information exists somewhere, in an application, a spreadsheet, a field report, or a map, but nobody can see it all in one place. Valortek was set up to fix that.

Our work covers the whole path from raw data to a clear answer: collecting and cleaning the data, adding location, analyzing it, and presenting it in dashboards, maps, and 3D views that people actually open.

What makes us different: we don't sell a single software package. We look at the data you already have, choose the tools that fit it, and build a solution your team can run and extend after we hand it over.
