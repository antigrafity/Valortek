How We Choose

If the systems and licenses you already pay for can do the job, we build on them first.

Open Standards

We use standard formats and interfaces, so you are never locked in to us or to a single vendor.

Right-Sized Cost

Licenses and servers are sized to your actual users and workload, with open-source options where they make sense.

Your Team Can Run It

We choose tools your staff can learn and maintain, and we document everything we build.
