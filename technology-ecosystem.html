Technology & Tools

The Right Tools for Your Data

We use well-known commercial and open-source tools for data, GIS, analytics, and visualization, and pick them based on your data, your team's skills, and your budget.

Technology Areas

What Sits Inside a Valortek Solution

Every solution is built from proven components in five layers, and we choose each one to fit how you already work.

Layer 1

Data Storage

Relational databases, spatial databases, data warehouses, and cloud or on-premise storage.

Layer 2

Data Integration

Pipelines and connectors that move data from business applications, files, and mobile forms.

Layer 3

GIS & Mapping

Desktop and web GIS platforms, base maps, and mobile field collection apps.

Layer 4

Analytics & Reporting

Reporting and dashboard tools, plus custom analytical models where needed.

Layer 5

3D & Visualization

3D modeling, digital twin, and interactive presentation technologies for web and large displays.

How We Choose

Four Rules for Technology Selection

Use What You Already Have

If your current databases or licenses can do the job, we build on them before recommending anything new.

Open Standards

We favor standard data formats and interfaces so your data can move between tools in the future.

Right-Sized Cost

Licensing and infrastructure are matched to your number of users and data volume, with open-source options where suitable.

Your Team Can Run It

We choose tools your staff can learn and maintain, and we document everything we build.
