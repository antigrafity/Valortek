Every Sector Asks Different Questions

The tools are the same, but every sector needs different answers. Here are a few examples of what we help each one figure out.

Sector Examples

What We Help Each Sector Answer

Property

Property & Real Estate

Which units are occupied, what needs maintenance, and how will the next development look on site? Asset registers, occupancy dashboards, and 3D project visualization.

Utilities

Energy & Utilities

Where is our network, what condition is it in, and where is demand growing? Network maps, maintenance history, and consumption analytics.

Telco

Telecommunications

Which areas are well served and where should we build next? Coverage and site maps combined with customer and revenue data.

Retail

Retail & Consumer

Which outlets perform best and where should the next one open? Branch performance dashboards and catchment-area analysis.

Agri

Plantation & Agriculture

How productive is each block, and which areas need attention this season? Land mapping, field data collection, and yield dashboards.

Logistics

Logistics & Distribution

Are our depots in the right places and are delivery targets being met? Depot coverage maps and service-level dashboards.

Health

Healthcare

How are our facilities performing and where are services most needed? Facility dashboards and service-area analysis, with patient data protected under UU PDP.

Public

Public Services

Where are public facilities, and are services reaching everyone? Land and facility registers, digital service forms, and public-service dashboards.
